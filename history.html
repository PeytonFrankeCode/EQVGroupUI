Our Story

History

The milestones that built the EQV platform, from founding in 2022 to trading on the NYSE.

2022

EQV Group founded

Jerry Silvey founds the EQV Group to acquire mature, long-life producing oil & gas assets across the overlooked basins of North America.

2022-2024

Building the portfolio

Fourteen acquisitions build the early platform to more than 1,600 oil & gas properties across 16 active basins, the foundation that the acquisitions below would more than double to over 3,500 properties today.

August 2024

EQV Ventures IPO: $350M

EQV Ventures Acquisition Corp., the group’s first special purpose acquisition company, prices its $350 million initial public offering on the NYSE.

Q4 2024

Delaware Basin entry, West Texas

EQV enters the Permian Basin with an operated, oil-weighted position in Reeves and Loving counties, Texas. Spanning 50+ horizontal wells targeting the Wolfcamp and Bone Spring formations, the asset established a modern development platform in one of North America’s most prolific oil basins.

Q4 2024

Texas Panhandle, Anadarko Basin

EQV acquires a long-life conventional portfolio of approximately 875 wellbores in the Anadarko Basin of the Texas Panhandle.

July 2025

EQV Ventures II: $460M

The group’s second SPAC closes an upsized $460 million IPO with partial exercise of the over-allotment option.

August 2025

Presidio agreement signed

Presidio Petroleum agrees to go public via business combination with EQV Ventures, creating a dividend-yield-driven public E&P at roughly $660 million enterprise value.

Q4 2025

East Texas & North Louisiana

EQV acquires more than 1,500 wells across East Texas and North Louisiana, anchored by stacked-pay natural gas production from the Cotton Valley, Travis Peak, Pettit, Hosston, and Haynesville formations. With more than 900 operated wells and field histories reaching back to the 1940s, the acquisition brought meaningful natural gas scale at a time of growing demand for reliable American energy.

Q4 2025

South Texas & Mississippi: Tuscaloosa Marine Shale

EQV expands along the Gulf Coast with nearly 150 wells across South Texas and southwest Mississippi. The portfolio pairs steady conventional natural gas production in Polk, Hardin, and Tyler counties, Texas, with oil production from the Tuscaloosa Marine Shale, one of the Gulf Coast’s promising emerging oil plays.

March 2026

Trading as NYSE: FTW

The Presidio business combination completes and the combined company begins trading on the NYSE under ticker FTW.

Q3 2026

Northwest Shelf, Southern New Mexico

In its largest acquisition to date, EQV adds approximately 1,600 producing wells across Eddy and Lea counties in southeastern New Mexico, on the Northwest Shelf of the Permian Basin. Producing from the Yeso, Paddock, Blinebry, and Grayburg-San Andres intervals, the acquisition more than doubled EQV’s gross operated production and establishes New Mexico as a cornerstone of the portfolio.
