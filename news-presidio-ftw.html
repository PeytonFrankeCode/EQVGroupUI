Presidio Production Company Completes Business Combination and Begins Trading on the NYSE Under Ticker “FTW”

March 5, 2026

FORT WORTH, Texas. Presidio Investment Holdings LLC (“Presidio” or the “Company”), an oil and gas operator focused on the acquisition and optimization of producing oil and natural gas assets, completed its business combination with EQV Ventures Acquisition Corp. The combined company began trading on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol “FTW.”
