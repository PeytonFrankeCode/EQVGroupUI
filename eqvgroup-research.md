Research dossier for the redesign of [eqvgroup.com](https://eqvgroup.com).
Compiled 2026-07-02.

**How this was gathered:** the live site returns HTTP 403 to automated
tools (bot protection) and this workspace's network policy also blocks
direct access, so everything below comes from search-engine snippets of the
site's own pages, SEC filings, press releases, and third-party records.

- ✅ **From the site itself** (via search snippets of eqvgroup.com pages)
- 🟡 **From reliable press/SEC sources** (accurate, but may not match the site's wording)

## 1. Company overview

- ✅ Positioning statement: *"The EQV Group seeks to acquire mature,
  long-life and low-decline upstream producing oil & gas assets and related
  midstream infrastructure within the overlooked basins of North America
  and Europe."*
- ✅ Mission: *"to provide unprecedented direct access to a diversified
  portfolio of proved developed producing assets in a highly optimized,
  transparent, and cost-effective structure."*
- 🟡 Founded in 2022 by Jerome ("Jerry") Silvey.
- 🟡 Active acquirer of producing reserves: **14 acquisitions completed**,
  currently managing/operating **1,800+ wells across 10 states** (an
  earlier description says "interest in some 1,600 wells in 10 states and
  16 basins" — numbers have grown over time; get current figures from client).
- 🟡 Current operated portfolio focused on **western Oklahoma and the Texas
  Panhandle**.
- 🟡 Production: **25,233 boe/d net in 2024, 25% liquids**.

## 5. Page content details

### Owner Relations (`/owners`, `/eqv-operating`)

  interest owners and operating partners."*

### FAQ (`/faq`) — topics confirmed present
- ✅ **Division orders:** keep one copy, remit an executed copy back to EQV
  Operating. Division orders are sent only when an interest is calculated
  for a well's initial disbursement or affected by an account change. Upon
  completion of a transfer, an amended division order is issued reflecting
  the change.
- ✅ **First payment:** after first production, a revenue check is issued
  within the state's applicable statutory period for initial production
  revenue disbursement.
- ✅ **Payment delays:** most common cause is the account balance not
  reaching the required minimum under the lease or applicable law.
- ✅ **Lowering the minimum:** written request with owner number, desired
  minimum (as low as **$25**), and signature.
- ✅ **Missing checks:** if 20+ days since issuance, contact Owner Relations.
- ✅ **1099s:** issued to royalty owners who received a gross amount of
  **$10 or greater** during the tax year.
- ✅ **Address changes:** if address changed since a disbursement, contact
  the Owner Relations number.

## 6. Design observations (current site)

- Template-based (client's words: "just a template"), likely Squarespace.
- Serves **two distinct audiences** with very different needs:
  1. **Institutional/investor audience** — mission, strategy, SPAC news.
  2. **Royalty & interest owners** — often older, less technical users who
     need to find a phone number, FAQ answer, or mailing instructions fast.
  The redesign should keep owner-facing paths (Owner Relations, FAQ,
  Changes to Your Account) prominent and legible.
- SEO: keep the existing URL paths (or 301-redirect them) — `/owners`,
  `/faq`, `/news`, `/contact`, `/changes-to-your-account`, `/eqv-operating` —
  since they're indexed and linked from press coverage.
