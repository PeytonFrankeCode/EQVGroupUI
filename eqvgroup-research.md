## 4. Current design (what "make it cleaner" is starting from) ✅

- **Brand color:** vivid royal blue (≈ #2B3BEA-#3B4FE4 range) used for the
  announcement banner, CONTACT button, stat numbers, links, and PDF icons -
  on white with a near-black charcoal for dark sections.
- **Logo:** "EQV" wordmark, dark charcoal; the Q has a magnifier-style tail.
  White version not seen; footer uses the dark version on light grey.
- **Typography:** tall condensed uppercase sans (Oswald-like) for headings;
  a plain system-ish sans for body copy.
- **Imagery:** golden-hour aerial landscape photos/video (mountain valley
  overlooks, Utah-looking, not oilfield imagery).
- **Layout quirks worth fixing in the redesign:**
  - Enormous empty hero bands on interior pages (EQV OPERATING / CONTACT US
    headline floats in a huge grey void)
  - News list is duplicated on the home page at near-full length
  - Document download cards are plain grey boxes with inconsistent alignment
  - Contact page's two-column layout wastes space; regional email split is
    easy to miss
  - Line-length and hierarchy issues throughout (hero paragraph is one long
    all-caps block)

## 5. Client design directives for the redesign

Decisions the client has already made, these override whatever the current
site does:

1. **Motto placement:** the Latin motto **"Esse Quam Videri"** ("to be,
   rather than to seem", the phrase EQV is named after) is a dark-mode
   easter egg. It must NOT be a background/watermark treatment, when dark
   mode is on, display it as a small tagline **directly under the EQV logo**
   in the header; keep it hidden in light mode.

## 6. Company background (press/SEC research) 🟡

- Founded 2022 by **Jerome ("Jerry") Silvey** (CEO & Chairman; ex-Magnetar
  Capital Energy & Infrastructure 2016-2022, ex-RBC Capital Markets).
- Leadership per SPAC filings: **Tyson Taylor** (President & CFO, ex-Magnetar
  counsel), **Mickey Raney** (COO, co-founder Impact Energy Partners),
  **Grant Raney** (EVP, ex-Chesapeake land), **Danny Murray** (CAO &
  Secretary, ex-Chesapeake tax/accounting).
- Scale per press: 14 acquisitions; 1,800+ wells managed/operated across 10
  states (site says "1,600+ oil & gas properties, 16 active basins, 50+
  professionals"); ~25,233 boe/d net in 2024 (25% liquids); operated
  portfolio focused on western Oklahoma / Texas Panhandle.
- **Entities:** EQV Group (parent) · EQV Operating LLC (operator, TX RRC
  #101634) · EQV Resources LLC (sold Panhandle assets to Presidio for ~$59M)
  · EQV Ventures Acquisition Corp. (SPAC I → merged with Presidio; NYSE: FTW
  since 3/5/26) · EQV Ventures Acquisition Corp. II (SPAC II; $460M raised;
  NYSE: EVAC/EVACU).
