FAQ

Frequently asked questions

General Division Orders Joint Interest Billing Revenue Tax

General

How do I change my address, name, or ownership details?

Account changes must be requested in writing with supporting documentation. Use the forms above, and see the Account Changes section for how to submit them. Some jurisdictions may have different legal requirements that could require additional or different documentation.

Who do I contact about my account?

Reach the Owner Relations team at Ownerrelations@eqvoperating.com or (405) 870-3786. Include your owner number on all correspondence.

Division Orders

What should I do when I receive a division order?

Upon examination of your division order, keep one copy for your records and remit an executed copy back to EQV Operating.

When should I expect to receive a division order?

You should only receive a division order if your interest has been calculated for a well’s initial disbursement, or if it has been affected by a change that has occurred on your account. Upon completion of a transfer, an amended division order is issued reflecting the change.

Joint Interest Billing

Who do I contact about joint interest billing?

For questions about joint interest billing statements, contact Owner Relations at Ownerrelations@eqvoperating.com with your owner number and the property involved.

Revenue

When will I receive my first revenue check?

Following a well’s first production, a revenue check will be issued within the state’s applicable statutory period regarding initial production revenue disbursement.

Why haven’t I received a payment?

The most common reason for account delays is that your account balance hasn’t reached the required minimum amount set up for your account under your lease or applicable law.

Can I lower my minimum payment amount?

Yes. Submit a written request that includes your owner number, the amount you’d like your minimum lowered to (as low as $25), and your signature.

My payment was issued but never arrived. What now?

If it has been at least 20 days since your payment was issued and you still haven’t received it, contact Owner Relations and we’ll research and reissue it as needed.

Tax

Will I receive a 1099?

1099s are issued to royalty owners who have received a gross amount of $10 or greater during the tax year.
