EQV Overview

The EQV Group seeks to acquire mature, long-life and low-decline upstream producing oil & gas assets and related midstream infrastructure within the overlooked basins of North America and Europe.

Our mission is to provide unprecedented direct access to a diversified portfolio of proved developed producing assets in a highly optimized, transparent, and cost-effective structure.

The EQV Platform

One platform, across the energy landscape

EQV Group is the parent of an integrated energy platform — from operating producing assets and serving interest owners, to the public acquisition vehicles that fund the group’s growth.
